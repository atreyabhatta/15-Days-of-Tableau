Day 04 - Dimensions vs Measures, Aggregation

Topics Covered:
- Dimensions (qualitative, categorical fields)
- Measures (quantitative, numerical fields)
- Blue Pills vs Green Pills
- Discrete vs Continuous
- Default Aggregation (SUM, AVG, COUNT, etc.)
- Changing Aggregation Type
- Converting a Dimension to a Measure (and vice versa)
- How Dimensions "de-aggregate" a Measure

Practice:
- Identified Dimensions (Category, Region, Customer Name) vs Measures
  (Sales, Profit, Quantity) in Sample Superstore dataset
- Dragged a Measure (Sales) alone into the view - saw it aggregate to one
  single SUM value
- Dragged a Dimension (Category) alongside it - watched the measure
  "de-aggregate" into per-category totals
- Practiced changing default aggregation from SUM to AVG, then to COUNT
  DISTINCT on a Measure
- Compared Blue pill (discrete, creates headers) vs Green pill
  (continuous, creates an axis) behavior on Rows/Columns
- Manually converted a Dimension to a Measure and observed the color/behavior change

Key Takeaways:
- Dimensions slice/segment data (they set the "level of detail")
- Measures are what get aggregated (Sum, Avg, Count, etc.)
- Blue = Discrete (headers), Green = Continuous (axis) - this determines how
  a field renders in the view, independent of Dimension/Measure status
- Getting Dimension/Measure and Discrete/Continuous confused is one of the
  most common beginner mistakes in Tableau
